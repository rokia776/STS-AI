# Horizon Delta University: Tuition, Fees and Scholarships 2026-2027

Document ID: HDU-TS-2026-01 | Version 2.0 | Effective: Fall 2026 | Currency: Egyptian pound (EGP) | Purpose: RAG chatbot testing

> **FICTIONAL UNIVERSITY DISCLAIMER.** Horizon Delta University (HDU) is fictional. All amounts, dates and rules below are invented for software demonstration and testing only.

**This document replaces the 2025-2026 tuition policy (Version 1.0) for all semesters from Fall 2026 onward.** The 2025-2026 policy is archived and must not be used for 2026-2027 questions.

## 1. Tuition per credit hour

Tuition depends on the student's faculty and is charged per registered credit hour.

| Faculty | Tuition per credit hour |
|---|---|
| Computing and AI (CAI) | 4,200 EGP |
| Business (BUS) | 3,600 EGP |
| Engineering (ENG) | 4,800 EGP |

Tuition for a semester = registered credit hours multiplied by the tuition per credit hour of the student's faculty. The number of credit hours of each course is listed in the Course Catalog.

## 2. Fees (not discounted by any scholarship)

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Registration fee | 1,500 EGP per Fall or Spring semester | 750 EGP for the Summer semester. Never refunded. |
| Student activities fee | 400 EGP per Fall or Spring semester | Not charged in Summer. Never refunded. |
| Lab fee | 600 EGP per course that has a lab | Courses with a lab are CAI-301 and ENG-102. |
| Late payment fee | 500 EGP | Charged when the payment deadline is missed. |

Housing and transport are not part of tuition or fees; they are described in the Student Services guide.

## 4. Installment plan

An installment plan is available when the semester tuition is at least 20,000 EGP.

- A 3% administrative charge is added to the tuition.
- The tuition plus the 3% charge is split into two equal installments.
- The first installment, together with **all fees**, is due on the payment deadline.
- The second installment is due at the end of week 6 of the semester (Thursday 29 October 2026 for Fall 2026, Thursday 25 March 2027 for Spring 2027).

## 5. Scholarships and discounts

| Scholarship | Discount on tuition | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Entry Excellence Scholarship | 50% | Admission score of 95% or above. Applies to the first two semesters. Renewed each year while the CGPA is at least 3.5 and the student is full-time (at least 12 credit hours). |
| Merit Scholarship | 25% | For the next semester when the CGPA is at least 3.7 at the end of the previous semester and at least 12 credit hours were completed in it. |
| Sibling Discount | 10% | For the second and any further sibling who is enrolled at HDU at the same time. |
| Need-Based Grant | 10% to 30% | Apply with family income documents by 15 August (for Fall) or 15 January (for Spring). |

Rules:

1. Discounts apply to **tuition only**, never to fees, housing or transport.
2. The Entry Excellence Scholarship and the Merit Scholarship cannot be combined; the larger one applies.
3. All other discounts can be combined, but the **total discount can never exceed 60% of tuition**.
4. **Academic probation:** merit-based scholarships (Entry Excellence and Merit) are suspended while the student is on academic probation, as defined in the Student Handbook. The Sibling Discount and the Need-Based Grant are not affected. A suspended scholarship can be reinstated from the semester after the student returns to good standing.

### Worked example

A Computing and AI student registers for 12 credit hours with no lab course and holds the Entry Excellence Scholarship. Tuition is 12 x 4,200 = 50,400 EGP. The 50% scholarship reduces it to 25,200 EGP. Fees are 1,500 + 400 = 1,900 EGP. The total to pay is 27,100 EGP.

## 6. Refunds

### 6.1 Complete withdrawal from the university

| When the student withdraws | Tuition refund |
|---|---|
| Before the semester starts | 100% |
| Weeks 1 and 2 | 80% |
| Weeks 3 and 4 | 50% |
| Week 5 or later | 0% |

Fees are never refunded. Approved refunds are paid by bank transfer within 15 working days.

### 6.2 Dropping a single course

If a student drops one course during the add/drop period, the tuition of that course is removed in full (credit hours of the course multiplied by the tuition per credit hour). After the add/drop deadline there is no tuition refund for a single course; a withdrawal (W) gives no refund.
